How it works

  1. 1 Pick

    Leaf through the catalog and pick a piece. Tap Own it now! and the box lands on your floor.

  2. 2 Unpack

    Lay the panels out. Drag a part to move it, circle its ring to turn it, lift it with a second finger.

  3. 3 Build

    Follow the picture manual. Seat the dowels, tighten the cams, drive the screws and slide the drawer home.

Made a mistake? Nothing is ever glued: pull the dowel, back the screw out, turn the cam open. Your room saves itself as you go, right there in your browser.
