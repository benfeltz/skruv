Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short: Skruv collects nothing about you. Your room is saved on your own device and never sent to us.

This policy covers the Skruv game, whether you play it at skruv.site or in the Skruv app, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. Skruv has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. The game does not send your progress, your device's details or anything else about you to us or to anyone else.

Your saved room

So you can pick up where you left off, the game saves your room (which pieces you've bought, where each part is and how far each build has got) in your browser's local storage, or in the app's own storage. That save stays on your device. We can't see it, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the website data for skruv.site in your browser's settings, or by deleting the app.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Skruv contains no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no third-party tracking of any kind. Any performance readout you may see in a test build is computed on your device and never leaves it.

Haptics and device features

On devices that support it, Skruv gives a short vibration when a part seats or a screw goes home. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Hosting

This website and the browser version of the game are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, to deliver it and keep the service secure. We don't receive or use that information. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, and we never share it or add you to a mailing list.

Children

Skruv is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@skruv.site.
