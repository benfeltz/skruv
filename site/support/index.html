Support & FAQ

Playing

What do I need to play?

A recent iPhone or Android phone with Safari or Chrome. Open skruv.site/play and you're in. There's nothing to download and no account.

Skruv also runs in a desktop browser with a mouse, though it is made first for touch.

How do I move, turn and lift parts?

On a phone:

  • Look around: drag one finger on empty space to orbit; pinch to zoom and pan.
  • Pick a part: tap it. Rings appear around it; drag a ring to turn the part.
  • Move it: drag the part across the floor.
  • Lift it: while dragging, slide a second finger up or down, or use the lift line at the side of the screen.

On a computer: left-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Hold Shift to raise the part you're holding, and Shift + scroll to set its height.

The manual has a controls page too: it's one of the first pages you'll flip past.

How do the tools and fasteners work?
  • Dowels and pins: drop one over its hole and it seats; tap the part to push it home.
  • Screws, bolts and cams: bring the right tool to the fastener, then circle the tool (or its ring) to turn it, or hold it still to drive it home.
  • Legs on studs: set the leg on its stud and circle the leg itself, no tool needed.
  • Drawers: drag the drawer along its runners. Pull it past the end and it comes off.

Everything works backwards too: pull a dowel out, back a screw out, turn a cam open.

How do I switch between pieces, or start a piece over?

Each piece you've bought keeps its own manual in the pocket at the end of the manual band. Tap the pocket to spread the covers, then tap a cover to switch to that piece. Your other builds stay exactly where you left them.

Start over: open the menu (☰, top left) and press and hold Repack until its bar fills. Everything goes back in the box.

Remove a piece: spread the covers, then press and hold the open manual's cover. Let go early to cancel.

To buy another piece, open the catalog from its thumbnail in the top-right corner.

Saving & installing

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your room saves itself as you build, in your browser's storage on this device. Close the tab and come back later and it's all there.

A few things to know:

  • The save lives on this device and this browser only. It doesn't sync to your other devices.
  • In a private / incognito window, the save is gone when the window closes.
  • Clearing your browser's website data for skruv.site deletes your room.
Can I put Skruv on my Home Screen?

Yes, and it's the best way to play: it opens full screen with no browser bars in the way.

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): open skruv.site/play, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): open skruv.site/play, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Troubleshooting

The game is slow, or the screen stays blank.
  • Update your browser and your phone's system software. Skruv needs WebGL, which every current browser has.
  • Close other tabs and apps: 3D and physics use a lot of memory.
  • Low Power Mode on iPhone limits the frame rate. Turn it off for smoother play.
  • On a computer, make sure hardware acceleration is on in your browser's settings.
  • Still blank? Reload the page. Your room is saved, so you won't lose anything.
The page scrolls or zooms when I try to move a part.

Some browser gestures, like swiping from the very edge of the screen, belong to the browser and not the game. Start your drags a little in from the edges, or add Skruv to your Home Screen (above) so it runs full screen.

How do I report a bug or suggest a piece of furniture?

Email support@skruv.site. For a bug, tell us your phone model, your browser, which piece you were building, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Still stuck?

Write to support@skruv.site. We read every message.
